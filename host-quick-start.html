Host Quick Start

Use this one-page guide to set up, score, and close the game smoothly.

Before People Join
  1. Run python3 -m http.server 8765, then open http://127.0.0.1:8765/index.html.
  2. Set two to five teams from Teams.
  3. Confirm the projector view and team names.
  4. Turn sound on only if the room can hear it clearly.
Host Rhythm
  1. Pick a category and value.
  2. Read the clue, then reveal the response.
  3. Score the selected team or close as No Score.
  4. Use Host Details after reveal to connect the learning point.

Scoring Rules

Correct

Adds the clue value and makes that team active.

Incorrect

Subtracts the clue value and opens a steal unless all teams have tried.

No Score

Marks the clue used without changing points.

Undo

Restores the last scoring or close action.

Host Notes

Intro

Welcome teams. Today we are practicing how Coca-Cola teams move from burden signals to practical food safety solutions.

Scoring

Reveal the response before scoring. Use host discretion for equivalent answers and Final rubric calls; do not change point values during play.

Steal

After a miss, choose another team for one optional steal or close the clue with no score.

Confidentiality

Keep examples generic. Do not share formulas, KORE details, audit results, real site layouts, supplier names, active incidents, or unreleased business information.

Final Jeopardy Flow

  1. Open Final Jeopardy when the board is done or when time is running short.
  2. Enter wagers. Revealing the final clue locks wagers and team count.
  3. Reveal the final response, then mark Full, Half, or Miss for each wagered team.
  4. Teams with zero wagers are auto-reviewed and can go straight to results.

Debrief Cue

Ask each team for one action that moves food safety from burden to solution: a signal they will watch, a risk they will prevent, or a habit they will reinforce.
