Food Safety Passport Challenge

Host Guide

Use these materials for a five-station Coca-Cola plant or bottling-site event. Keep each station to 3-5 minutes and stamp the passport after coaching the associate through the activity.

  1. Can You Spot the Risk? Earn a stamp by finding five credible risks.
  2. Perfect Product Match. Earn a stamp by matching production steps to controls.
  3. Myth or Fact. Earn a stamp by answering four of six correctly.
  4. Allergen & Label Alert. Earn a stamp by identifying one missing label or allergen risk.
  5. Raise the Red Flag. Earn a stamp by choosing the right action for three scenarios.
